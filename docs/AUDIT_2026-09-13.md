# SilentVoice AI test observations, 13 September 2026

This record documents observations made during local browser testing on the
`partner-aware-mode` branch. It records what was observed. It does not assign
causes or implement fixes.

## 1. Assistance confirmation opened unexpectedly during gaze testing

During gaze testing, the assistance confirmation dialog opened without the
tester intentionally requesting assistance.

The dialog displayed:

`Send a request for assistance?`

No assistance request was sent. The confirmation step remained in place and
prevented an unintended request from being sent.

At the time of the observation, browser developer tools were open and the
application viewport was narrower than during normal full-width use. No cause
has been established.

Status: open observation. No fix made in this change.

## 2. `/generate` returned HTTP 500

During the same browser testing session, the browser console showed a failed
request to:

`POST /generate`

The response status was HTTP 500.

This is the standard Generate AI Phrase path and is separate from the
Partner-Aware `/partner-aware/replies` endpoint.

The cause was not investigated during this test and is not inferred here.

Status: open issue requiring separate diagnosis before tester release. No fix
made in this change.

## 3. Partner-Aware temporary-card removal and scanner focus

A browser observer recorded 12 `.comm-card` elements before selection of a
Partner-Aware suggestion.

The first temporary Partner-Aware suggestion was then selected through a
scripted DOM click during the measurement.

After selection:

- the card count changed from 12 to 9
- scanner focus remained at index 5
- focus index `-1` was not observed during the measured transition
- index 5 remained a valid index among the 9 remaining cards

This verifies that removal of the three temporary Partner-Aware suggestion
cards did not leave the measured scanner focus on an invalid index during that
transition.

Post-removal scanner movement was not measured. No claim is made here about
scanner advancement after the temporary cards had been removed.

Status: measured transition recorded. No fix made in this change.
