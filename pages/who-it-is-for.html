Who it is for

People whose speech does not carry, and the person beside them.

Speaking can become difficult or impossible for many reasons, for a short time or for good. Choosing from prepared phrases is one of the oldest ways to be heard.

SilentVoice AI does not replace specialist communication systems. It asks a narrower question: how much can be done with a computer someone already owns.

Ordinary equipment

A computer with a camera. Nothing worn, nothing to install.

A person and a helper

Set up together in about half a minute.

Honest on bad days

When the signal is not clear enough, it says so and selects nothing.
