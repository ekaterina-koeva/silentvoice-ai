Who it is for

People whose speech does not carry, and the person beside them.

The situation

Speaking can be difficult or impossible after a stroke, with cerebral palsy, and after losing the voice to illness or surgery. Choosing from a set of prepared phrases is one of the oldest and most dependable ways to be heard.

SilentVoice AI does not try to replace the specialist communication systems that many people already rely on. It asks a narrower question: how much can be done with a computer someone already owns.

Built for

One person, one assistant

A person and the assistant who sets it up with them. Not a ward, not a fleet of devices, and not a dashboard for someone else to watch.

Ordinary equipment

A laptop or a computer with a camera. No eye tracking hardware, nothing worn, nothing to install.

People who are not developers

Setting it up is three dots and half a minute. Everything the system decides is written on the screen in plain words.

Days when it does not work

Light changes, positions change, and some days the signal is not good enough. The system says so and steps out of the way rather than guessing.
