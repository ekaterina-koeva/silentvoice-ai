Early stage prototype. It is not registered or clinically validated.

Speak with your eyes.

Choose a phrase by looking to one side. The computer says it out loud.

For people whose speech does not carry. Runs in a web browser with an ordinary camera.

See how it works Source code

How it works

Calibrate

Look at three dots for about half a minute.

Look

Phrases light up one at a time. Hold a look to one side to choose.

Be heard

The computer speaks the phrase.

Where it is today

Works

  • Choosing by eyes or by touch
  • Calibration for each person
  • Camera images are processed in the browser

Not yet

  • There is no confirmation that a help request was received
  • Timings cannot be adjusted yet
