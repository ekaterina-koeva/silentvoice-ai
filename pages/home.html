Early stage prototype, under regulatory assessment

Say what you need, with your eyes.

SilentVoice AI shows everyday phrases on screen. The person chooses one by looking to one side, or by touch, and the device speaks it aloud.

It runs in a browser on an ordinary computer with a camera. Nothing is worn and nothing is installed.

In short

01

Calibrate

Three dots, about half a minute. The system measures how that particular person looks left, at the middle and right.

02

Choose

Cards highlight one at a time. A held look to one side selects, a look to the other side moves on.

03

Be heard

The phrase is spoken aloud in a voice chosen on the device.

The longer version, with what it refuses to do

Current status

Early stage prototype, under regulatory assessment.

SilentVoice AI is an early stage assistive communication prototype currently undergoing regulatory assessment. It is not registered or clinically validated. Public access to the working prototype is restricted while that assessment is in progress.

It must not be relied on as the sole means of emergency communication, and it does not contact emergency services.

What can be checked

There are no testimonials here, because there are no users yet.

Nobody outside the project has used SilentVoice AI. Rather than borrow credibility we do not have, here is what anyone is free to open and verify.

The development history is public

Every change since 19 May 2026, with messages that say what was measured and how it was verified, including the defects found and corrected along the way.

Tests run on every change

Automated tests run through GitHub Actions on every change to the main branch, and the results are public.

The measurements are dated and repeatable

The diagnostic tool that produced them is in the repository, so the measurement can be repeated on another camera by anyone.

What is missing is written down too

No users, no clinical validation, no formal usability testing. The open questions are recorded rather than left out.
