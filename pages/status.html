Current status

What is true today, including the parts that are not finished.

The position

Early stage prototype, under regulatory assessment.

SilentVoice AI is an early stage assistive communication prototype currently undergoing regulatory assessment. It is not registered or clinically validated. Public access to the working prototype is restricted while that assessment is in progress.

It must not be relied on as the sole means of emergency communication, and it does not contact emergency services.

What that means

The working interface is behind an invitation code and a password. Advisers, professionals and invited testers can be given access. The public pages stay public.

No formal usability testing and no clinical validation have been carried out. Nobody outside the project has used the prototype. When that changes, the results will be published with dates, including the parts that did not work.

The help button speaks a request for help aloud on the device. It does not notify anyone and it does not contact any emergency service. An alert that reaches a chosen person is being designed, and it will not contact emergency services either.

Nothing is for sale. There is no payment system.

Where the data goes

Camera frames are processed inside the browser and never leave the device. Nothing about a face is uploaded or stored.

Three things do leave the device, and the interface names each one where it happens. Generating a phrase with AI sends the selected words to an AI provider. Some speaking voices are produced on a Microsoft server, which sends the phrase text there. A browser without built in speech falls back to the server for speech.

The calibration profile and the choice of voice are kept in the browser on that device. These public pages store nothing at all.
