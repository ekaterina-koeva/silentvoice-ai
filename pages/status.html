Current status

What is true today, including what is not finished.

Early stage prototype.

SilentVoice AI is not registered, not certified and not clinically validated. Its regulatory classification has not been determined.

The help button speaks a request aloud on the device. It does not notify anyone and it does not contact emergency services. Do not rely on it in an emergency.

Testing

In September 2026 four test participants without a technical background tried the prototype. A problem they found was corrected and retested, see issue #2. This was informal usability testing. There are no users and there has been no clinical testing.

Your data

Camera video is processed in the browser and never leaves the computer.

Two things can leave it, and the screen says so when they do: the chosen words, when an AI phrase is requested, and the phrase text, when an online speaking voice is used.

Nothing is for sale and there is no payment system.
