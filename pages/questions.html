Questions

Short answers, including the awkward ones.

What do I need?

A computer or laptop with a camera and a current browser. Nothing to wear and nothing to install.

Does it work on a phone?

Not yet. It is built for a computer screen.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no payment system.

Is it a medical device?

Its regulatory classification has not been determined. It is not registered, not certified and not clinically validated.

Can it call for help?

No. The help button speaks a request aloud on the device. It does not notify anyone and it does not contact emergency services.

Why is the working prototype closed?

Because it is an early prototype that has not been clinically validated. Access is given to invited people who know its limits.

How do I get access?

Write using the contact form.
