Roadmap

What is done and what comes next. No dates are promised.

  1. Done Calibration for each person, selection by gaze or touch, a pause when the person changes position, choice of voice, a camera that starts only on request, and invited access.
  2. Done Corrections found in testing, including automatic scanning that now pauses while the person is looking to move.
  3. Next Adjustable timings, moving between cards with a keyboard or switch, and a help alert that reaches a chosen person.
  4. Next Editing the phrase cards, a privacy notice and an accessibility statement.
  5. Later Accounts and payments. None of this is built.
