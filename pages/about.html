Founder and developer

Ekaterina Koeva

Ekaterina Koeva founded SilentVoice AI and began developing the project in May 2026. Her work brings together Python, FastAPI, JavaScript, MediaPipe Face Mesh, browser speech technology, gaze calibration, automated testing and accessibility focused interaction design.

She leads the product direction, system design, implementation, testing and technical documentation. The public GitHub repository records the development process through dated commits, measurements, identified defects and corrections.

Why she started

SilentVoice AI began with a practical question: could an ordinary webcam and browser help a person select and speak everyday phrases without relying on touch or dedicated eye tracking hardware?

Ekaterina started testing this idea by building a browser based prototype that translates deliberate gaze movements into phrase selection and speech. Her aim is to create a communication route that works on widely available equipment and responds safely when the gaze signal is unclear.

What makes SilentVoice AI different

SilentVoice AI runs directly in the browser with a standard camera. Camera frames are processed on the device and are not uploaded or stored.

Gaze control is calibrated for each individual person. A quality gate checks whether the measured directions are sufficiently separated before gaze selection starts. When the system cannot distinguish the direction reliably, it selects nothing.

The engineering process is public. Measurements, failures, corrections and test results are recorded so the development can be independently reviewed.

The working prototype remains restricted to invited access while accessibility, safety and regulatory preparation continue.
