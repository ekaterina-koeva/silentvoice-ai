What she has built

A gaze reading that measures, not guesses. In August 2026 she found by measurement that the original gaze formula cancelled most of its own signal, replaced it with one shared axis for both eyes, and added calibration for each person. See the change

A system that refuses when it is unsure. An unclear reading selects nothing, and selection pauses when the person moves out of position. See the change

Honest failures. When the AI provider failed on the live site, the screen showed a sentence the person had not chosen. She traced the cause and made the screen report the failure instead. See the change

Privacy by default. The camera starts only when the person presses the button, and the face tracking library is stored in the project instead of loaded from another site. See the change

Testing with people. In September 2026 four test participants without a technical background tried the prototype. She corrected the problem they found and one of them retested it. See issue #2

Why she started

A practical question: can an ordinary webcam and browser let a person choose and speak everyday phrases without touch and without special eye tracking hardware?

She built the prototype to find out, and made it refuse to select anything when the gaze signal is unclear.
